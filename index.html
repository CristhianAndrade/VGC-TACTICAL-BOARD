VGC Tactical Board

Strategic Planning Tool for Pokémon VGC

The VGC Tactical Board is a specialized tool built for competitive Pokémon (VGC) players. It focuses on board positioning and spatial tactics, allowing trainers to simulate switches, predict movements, and map out win conditions in the Doubles format.
